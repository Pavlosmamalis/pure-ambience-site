Out now · Pure Ambience

Four engines. One signal. Built for the dark side of psy.

Pure Ambience is a four-engine synthesizer plugin (VST3/AU, Mac & Windows) built around a simple idea: most synths sound clean because they're trying to. Pure Ambience is built to sound alive.

MASTER · EQ / COMP / WIDTH / DRIVE

A real capture of the plugin running in FL Studio. Every screenshot here is the actual software.

01

Bass

Twisted, swampy low end. Twist, the one-knob drive and filter growl control on every engine, is built from a real sound-design trick, not a stock saturator.

02

Melody

20 character presets (Crystal Bell, Koto, Warm Vox, Marimba, Sitar, and more), each with its own designed envelope.

03

Atmosphere

Space and air: pads and drones that open a mix up rather than filling it.

04

Nature

31 real field recordings (mammals, birds, insects, amphibians, weather), all public-domain NPS sources, keytracked and playable like a sampler.

Filters
Switchable filter type (Low / Band / High-pass) per engine

A/B compare

Master Drive
Saturation stage for extra headroom on hard-hitting, high-BPM material
