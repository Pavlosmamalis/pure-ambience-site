Who we are

Instruments grown, not designed.

PureAmbience is a plugin company for the dark end of electronic music. We work from one picture: a brutalist ruin that the forest is taking back, cast concrete and machined steel with moss coming up through the cracks. Every plugin should look like that and sound like it.

Heavy at the bottom

Low ends that go from a clean sub to a growl with one knob, built for the dark side of psy.

Alive at the edges

Real recordings from the wild, played from the keyboard, so the organic edge is part of the instrument.

Made to be seen

Screens that follow the sound, so you can see what a sound is doing and reach in to change it.

Press a key and something wakes under the concrete.
